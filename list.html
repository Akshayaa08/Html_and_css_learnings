Mythical Creatures

Dragon
Dragons A dragon is a mythical creature found in the folklore of cultures throughout the world. It is roughly serpentine in form, and often possesses features drawn from different animals, such as wings and claws. In nearly all cultures, it is conceived of as a powerful and awe-inspiring creature.
Phoenix
A phoenix most commonly means a legendary, immortal bird from ancient mythology that bursts into flames and is born again from its ashes.
Vampire
A vampire is a mythical, undead creature that survives by feeding on the vital life force or blood of the living. Rooted deeply in global folklore—especially in Eastern European traditions—the modern concept of the vampire combines ancient legends of nocturnal demons with 19th-century gothic literature.
Werewolf
werewolf (from Old English wer meaning "man" and wulf meaning "wolf"), or lycanthrope, is a mythical human who transforms into a wolf or a fierce wolf-like creature. This change can happen on purpose or by accident, often caused by a curse, magic, or a bite from another werewolf.
